- Result: a deterministic read-only assessment of a source-owned machine asset and active preventive plan under one tenant/site/time scope. It reports due/not due as advisory only and blocks stale, foreign, future-effective or contradictory evidence; it never computes a new due date or creates/approves maintenance work. The caller must supply expected asset and plan sources, and the records' provenance revisions must match their record revisions. A supported result carries the tenant/site/evaluation time and exact evidence IDs, revisions, hashes, observation and effective times and plan due time; a blocked result carries no evidence references. Source binding is a comparison against caller configuration, not proof the caller or CMMS payload was authenticated.
