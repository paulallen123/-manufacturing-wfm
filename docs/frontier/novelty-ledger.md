## Loss-prevention impairment hypothesis

If WFM connects authoritative protection-system impairment state, temporary loss-prevention control roles, current competence/availability, restoration plans and production commitments, it can identify earlier safe restoration opportunities that reduce impaired time and release more temporary-control labour capacity than they displace.

Simple alternatives: keep the current production/maintenance plan unchanged, or always restore immediately without considering displacement.

Success threshold: zero recommendations with missing mandatory controls, zero silent cross-site competence borrowing, at least 20% median reduction in temporary-control person-minutes in qualifying realistic replays, and no degradation of protected customer/process-safety/quality commitments.

Stop criterion: stop/redesign if authoritative impairment/policy/restoration evidence is unavailable, local policies cannot be represented safely, realistic replay causes material production harm, or the feature creates pressure to bypass PTW/fire-protection/process-safety authority.

Safety boundary: advisory only. It cannot approve/close permits, command protection or OT systems, waive safety controls, determine insurance coverage, or turn modelled exposure reduction into a premium/loss-probability claim.

Scale inversion: a one-person site may correctly receive "no safe internal solution" where policy requires a separate control role; a multi-site enterprise remains strictly site/policy scoped and never borrows competence silently.
